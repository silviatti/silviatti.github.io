I’m a Senior Lead Machine Learning Engineer at Upwork, working on search, recommendation, and matching for a marketplace with millions of active freelancers. I started in research, with a PhD in Computer Science focused on NLP, representation learning, and topic models. I care about the assumptions behind a model, whether an evaluation really measures the thing we care about, and what the results are telling us—not just whether a metric went up. Today, I work closer to the product: designing ranking and recommendation systems, running experiments, and bringing ML into production. I also like making knowledge reusable.
