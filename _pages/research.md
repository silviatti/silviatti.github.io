My PhD research explored natural language processing, representation learning, and topic models. I continue to value open source as a way to make research useful, reproducible, and easier to build on. I like projects that make difficult ideas easier to test, compare, and use.

## OCTIS

[OCTIS](https://github.com/MIND-Lab/OCTIS) is an open-source framework for training, evaluating, and comparing topic models. I designed and developed the project to support fairer comparisons through hyperparameter optimization. It has earned more than 800 GitHub stars.

## Contextualized Topic Models

[Contextualized Topic Models](https://github.com/MilaNLProc/contextualized-topic-models) combines contextual language-model representations with neural topic models. I collaborated with the MilaNLP Lab on the project and am a maintainer and contributor. The project has more than 1,300 GitHub stars.

## FashionCLIP

[FashionCLIP](https://github.com/patrickjohncyh/fashion-clip) explores vision-language learning for general fashion concepts. I contributed to research and evaluation work associated with the project; the project itself is led and maintained by its wider contributor community. Its models have reached more than 100 million downloads on Hugging Face.

## Research interests

I’m interested in representation learning, evaluation, recommendation, and the bridge between research results and production systems. See my [publications](/publications/) for the academic record and [talks](/talks/talks/) for selected presentations.
