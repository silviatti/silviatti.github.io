## Upwork

**Senior Lead Machine Learning Engineer**

At Upwork, I build intelligent systems for a large-scale freelance marketplace. My work focuses on search, recommendation, and matching: helping clients and freelancers find the right opportunities and connections.

I work from problem framing through model design, evaluation, experimentation, and deployment.

## What I focus on

- Recommendation and matching systems for a marketplace with millions of active freelancers.
- Experimentation and evaluation that connect model quality to marketplace engagement and freelancer earnings.
- Taking ML ideas from research prototypes to reliable production systems.

I care about the practical details: defining the right objective, designing trustworthy evaluations, and learning from experiments once a model meets the real world.
